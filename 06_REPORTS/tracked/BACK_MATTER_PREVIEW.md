### Africa

- **Akan** — Ghana, Côte d’Ivoire · 2 stories · map: Asante and the Akan lands
  - *Twi*
  - These are told out loud, at night, by anybody who can hold a room, and the audience interrupts with songs.
  - Ghana and eastern Côte d’Ivoire, in forest country where the spider in the story is one you can meet on a wall.
  - Anansi stories are still told and still published, and the Caribbean versions are related to them but are not the same stories.
- **Ancient Egyptian** — Nile valley · 3 stories · map: The Nile valley
  - *Ancient Egyptian*
  - These come from papyrus rolls, and several survive in a single copy, so what reaches us depends on which roll did not rot.
  - The Nile valley and the Red Sea coast, in a country whose whole shape is one river with desert on both sides.
  - The religion ended long ago and the writing stayed unreadable for fifteen centuries, until a soldier turned up a slab with three scripts on it.
- **Yoruba** — Yorubaland (southwestern Nigeria, Benin) · 2 stories · map: Yorubaland
  - *Yorùbá*
  - Named narrators and diviners told these to collectors in the twentieth century, and Yoruba scholars have been writing about their own tradition ever since.
  - Yorubaland — Ilé-Ifẹ̀, Òṣogbo and the towns around them — and, after the slave trade, cities on the far side of the Atlantic.
